# Configure rain and collision-driven splash emitters

Complete `configure(scene, target)` in `configure.js`. The browser particle runtime, rendering, floor and inspection controls are provided. `requirements.json` supplies this task's target values, passed as `target`. This is an emitter configuration and connection task.

- **R1 — Rain budget and visibility.** Set the existing `Rain` emitter's `amount`, `lifetime` and `preprocess` to the target values. Amount is the particle capacity; continuous emission rate is amount divided by lifetime, so collisions can reduce the live count. Keep its position at 0, -200. Set `collisionBaseSize` from `baseSize` and `visibilityRect` to [-900, -200, 1800, 900], relative to the emitter. The viewport covers 1800 by 900 world units.
- **R2 — Rain material and connection.** Give Rain its own `ParticleMaterial`. Use `emissionShape` equal to `box`, `boxHalfExtents` of [900, 0], direction [target wind, 1], the target `velocity` range, zero gravity and scale from `scaleMin` to 1. Preserve the supplied 45 degree spread. Enable `collision` equal to `hide` and `subEmitterMode` equal to `collision`; set `particlesPerCollision` from `burst`. Set `subEmitter` to the actual `Splash` emitter. The emission width is 1800, with 900 on either side of the origin.
- **R3 — Splash material.** Set Splash's amount from `splashAmount`, `explosiveness` to 1, visible on and independent emission off. Give it a separate material with `alignY` true, direction [0, -1], the supplied 5 degree spread, velocity from `splashVelocity`, gravity [0, target gravity] and scale from `splashScale`. Keep its provided lifetime of 1 second. A contact hides the rain drop and emits the configured burst immediately at that contact; droplets rise and fall under gravity.
- **R4 — Ground and integration.** Preserve the supplied floor polygon, collider spanning 1920 by 120 at top y=260, and floor occlusion polygon. Preserve the existing emitter objects and their names. Material objects and range arrays must be independent of each other and of the target data. Keep the provided real preprocessing, collision simulation, culling, velocity-aligned rendering, pause/run, 0.1 second step and repeatable reset.

Only edit `configure.js`. Keep other files unchanged and submit the full package with `index.html` as entry. Acceptance checks every task-specific parameter and reference connection, actual distribution and trajectories, contact bursts and capacity, warm startup, visible animation and reset. Configuration values that do not drive the effect are insufficient.
