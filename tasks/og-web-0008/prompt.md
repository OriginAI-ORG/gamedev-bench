# Kingdom Cards

Build a complete browser strategy game in which a real deck governs construction,recruitment,war and diplomacy against two active rival kingdoms. Deliver a self-contained local `index.html`. Supplied fantasy and parchment atlases are optional artwork.

- **R1 — Real deck and turns.** Start the player with 12 gold,8 food,8 troops and this 16-card deck in order: farm,farm,market,barracks,militia,militia,militia,guard,guard,raid,raid,assault,assault,truce,trade,betrayal. Cards have unique IDs. Shuffle with Fisher–Yates and unsigned 32-bit xorshift shifts 13,17,5,divided by 4294967296; default seed 42,zero becomes 1. Initialize player,red,blue in that order using a shared random stream. Each faction's initial deck is shuffled before drawing; then draw 5 player cards and 3 for each rival,from the end of the pile. Play at most 3 cards per player turn by clicking; charge real costs,apply effects and move played cards to discard. Reject invalid targets,unaffordable actions and further plays without consuming cards/resources. At turn end choose unplayed cards to keep or permanently trash; discard all other unplayed cards. Keep and trash choices must be valid,unique and disjoint. Kept cards remain in hand; draw up to 5 additional cards next turn. When draw is empty,shuffle the discard pile; do not duplicate held/trashed cards. If both piles are empty,stop drawing. Show hand,draw/discard counts,total live deck size and permanently trashed cards. There is no hand-size cap; a long hand may scroll.
- **R2 — Territory and production.** Use the connected map below. House Alder is player,Crimson Crown is red and Azure League is blue. Neutral holdings have no income recipient. Each owned holding produces its listed resources at turn end,before rival orders. Each territory can hold one farm,one market and one barracks; only the owner may build. Farms add 3 food per turn,markets 3 gold,barracks 2 troops and 2 permanent local defense. Structures remain on capture. A new building permanently adds one stronger card to its owner's discard: farm gives harvest,market gives tax,barracks gives veterans. Acquisitions use fresh IDs and are recorded. A successful capture adds siege. Building costs are paid immediately;production waits for turn end.

- **R4 — Real armies and diplomacy.** Attack only an enemy/neutral holding adjacent to any friendly holding. Choose an integer army from 1 to current troops; deduct the entire commitment before resolution. Strength is army plus the card bonus; strict strength greater than defense is required to capture. On a win return surviving committed troops,maximum of 0 and committed minus ceiling of opposition strength divided by 2. For a single attack opposition is target defense;on failure all committed troops are lost. Capture changes ownership and resets defense to 2 plus 2 if barracks remain. Truces prohibit attacks in both directions and decrement after each rival phase;renewing sets duration to 2. Trade requires a living rival with at least 6 gold and actually transfers both resources. Betrayal requires an active truce with the target's owner,cancels it and attacks;it is not a free attack against arbitrary enemies. Failed transactions have no partial effects.
- **R5 — Two active rivals and simultaneous orders.** Each rival starts with 10 gold,8 food,5 troops and the ordered deck militia,raid,farm,assault,market,guard,militia,raid. Each rival phase,after income,both living rivals plan from the same board/resources before either order resolves. Each plays at most one real hand card: prefer a winnable adjacent attack,considering neutral targets first,then player,then the other rival;within that priority sort by ascending defense and preserve map order for ties. Search hand order for a legal attack using all available troops. Otherwise try recruitment in hand order at its first holding,then a legal building in hand/holding order,then provisions. If none is legal,pass. Resolve planned non-attack effects and pay all commitments,then resolve attacks grouped by target. Multiple attackers contest against the original defender and each other: capture requires a unique highest attacker strength strictly above defense;ties leave the defender in control. The winner's casualty opposition is the greater of defense and the second attacker's strength;losing attackers lose all commitments. Only the winner gains a card. Resolve target groups in first-order appearance. Show actual rival orders,battles,territory growth and losses. After a nonterminal phase,discard remaining rival hands and draw 3 fresh cards for each living rival. Eliminated rivals stop acting. Do not replace rival play with cosmetic reports.
- **R6 — Complete campaign and history.** Begin at an illustrated title,then campaign turn 1. Winning means owning all 6 territories;losing means owning none. Check terminal conditions after a player card and after the complete rival phase. Stop further actions on a terminal result. Preserve a territory-history snapshot at campaign start,after each target's combat resolution and after each rival phase;include terminal outcome snapshots. Results show victory/defeat,turn,holdings,deck size,cards gained/trashed,resources and a navigable visual territory-history review. A new campaign resets all state. Use coherent parchment-and-ink art,illustrated decrees,terrain/castles/farms,ownership flags,resource icons,wax-seal turn controls,visible building/combat/trash/capture feedback,animated card/turn changes and ambient map life. At 1280 by 720 the initial hand,map,resources,deck/turn counts and action controls are readable without overlap. At 390 by 844 scrolling is allowed.
