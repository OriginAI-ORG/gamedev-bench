# Rule Rewrite assessment

BUILD gates the score on a working local browser launch. Total = BUILD × (0.15 mean(M1–M4) +0.35 mean(D1–D4) +0.15 mean(V1–V3) +0.35 mean(A1–A3)). Rate each of the14 retained criteria0–1 using evidence. Automatic checks are functional evidence; unreviewed artistic values stay null.

|ID|Criterion|Evidence / aggregation|
|---|---|---|
|M1|Grid movement pushes word blocks one tile,with boundaries,blocking and no overlapping words.|Independent atomic-chain and live-keyboard tests; maximum.|
|M2|Horizontal/vertical noun-verb-property sentences activate immediately and deactivate when broken.|Independent parser,conditional suffix and public/UI checks; maximum.|
|M3|STOP,WIN,PUSH,YOU,DEFEAT change actual object behavior.|Collision/priority/push tests and all-level replay; maximum. Fewer than3 working properties scores0.|
|M4|Reassignable YOU controls all matching objects,including several simultaneously.|Control-transfer and blocked-independent-player tests; maximum.|
|D1|At least20 distinct levels,with different layouts,word positions and solutions,grouped into worlds.|All20 solutions plus manual content review; maximum. Fewer than5 scores0;5–19 without meaningful variety cannot exceed0.5.|
|D2|Escalation from simple rules to breaking/reforming several,conditions,transformations and identity/conflict resolution.|Campaign and independent transformation/ON/HAS tests; manual puzzle-depth review; maximum.|
|D3|At least6 nouns,each represented as a word and object with a distinct recognizable icon.|Content/visual review; maximum. Fewer than3 scores0.|
|D4|Unlimited per-move undo and in-game reset restore complete prior states.|300-move undo and transformation/birth/death/result restoration tests; maximum. Reset alone at most0.5.|
|V1|Active sentences visibly distinguish their participating word blocks.|Active-word/UI checks and manual readability; maximum.|
|V2|At1280×720,terrain,objects,words and categories are legible and distinct.|Bounds/actual-pixel checks and manual readability; mean across demos.|
|V3|Styled title,level selection,completion and progression,including completed-level replay.|Navigation/demos; mean.|
|A1|Coherent whimsical minimalist art across tiles,objects,word blocks,backgrounds and UI.|Manual; mean.|
|A2|Illustrated noun icons,styled textured word tiles and textured board terrain.|Manual; mean.|
|A3|Smooth tile slides,rule-formation feedback,completion celebration and screen transitions.|Manual motion review; mean.|

Where full credit requires authored assets or styled presentation,pure geometry,solid fills,default controls,color swaps or text-only variants are capped at0.5 for the affected criterion. Supplying fixed campaign layouts is an explicit reproducibility change that removes some original level-design freedom; it does not remove the obligation to implement the full language engine,all interactions and complete presentation. Content and artistic scoring must acknowledge that change rather than claim an identical original-task score.
