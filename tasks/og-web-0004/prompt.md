# Rule Rewrite

Build a complete browser word-block puzzle game where moving the words changes the world's rules. Deliver a self-contained local `index.html` package without external services. Implement the supplied 20 hand-authored layouts in `levels.js`, preserving their objects, word positions and terrain. The optional illustrated atlas provides six noun icons and themed backgrounds; it is not a game implementation.

- **R1 — Grid movement and pushing.** Use the 12 by 9 campaign grid. Arrow keys, WASD and visible directional buttons move every controlled object by one tile. Word blocks are always pushable; objects become pushable through rules. Push entire chains atomically: if any member cannot move because of terrain, bounds or an immovable STOP object, that chain does not move. Word blocks cannot overlap one another. Objects without STOP or PUSH can share cells. Process leading controlled objects first, and never move an object twice in one input even if another controlled object pushes it. One blocked controlled object must not prevent independent controlled objects from moving. Reject diagonal/non-unit movement.
- **R2 — Dynamic sentences.** Parse word blocks horizontally left-to-right and vertically top-to-bottom. `NOUN IS PROPERTY` activates behavior, `NOUN IS NOUN` transforms objects, and `NOUN HAS NOUN` specifies inheritance on defeat. Support `NOUN ON NOUN IS PROPERTY` as a spatial condition: the subject must share its tile with a separate object of the condition noun. Parse the complete conditional sentence; its suffix must not also become an unconditional sentence. Reparse after every accepted move; breaking a sentence removes its effect immediately. Highlight every word participating in a valid sentence and display the active sentences.
- **R3 — Properties and control.** Implement `YOU`, `STOP`, `PUSH`, `WIN`, `DEFEAT` for all six nouns: `SWORD`, `SHIELD`, `HEART`, `SKULL`, `FIRE`, `ICE`. YOU controls every matching object, including multiple objects of the same noun and simultaneous different nouns. Moving YOU to another sentence transfers control and leaves previous objects inert. STOP blocks entry; PUSH allows an object to move even if it also has STOP, provided the push succeeds. A controlled object sharing a tile with DEFEAT is removed before victory is checked. Surviving YOU contacting WIN completes the level; a controlled object that itself has WIN also qualifies. If no controlled object remains, show a stranded outcome with undo/reset available.
- **R4 — Transformations, conditions and inheritance.** After each accepted move, apply all current noun transformations simultaneously for one stage: A→B→C becomes B this move and C on the next, not an uncontrolled recursive loop. An active identity sentence A IS A protects A from other transformations. Multiple distinct noun targets create matching objects at the old position. Initial layout loading parses properties and resolves contacts without advancing transformation time. Evaluate ON conditions from actual co-located objects, including changes caused by movement or transformation. On defeat, HAS creates the inherited noun at the defeated object's tile. Remove defeated objects, then recompute surviving control and victory, including newly inherited objects. Do not repeatedly process a new death/transformation cascade within the same move.
- **R5 — Undo and full campaign.** Preserve unlimited per-move history, including word/object positions, noun identities, births/deaths, counters and outcome. Undo works after winning or losing control. Blocked inputs do not add history. Reset restores the exact initial level and clears its history. The 20 supplied levels span Written paths, Changing hands, Living names, Conditional places and Afterlife. Their progression includes sentence assembly, breaking STOP/DEFEAT, PUSH, control transfer, multiple YOU, transformation, identity protection, staged chains, ON conditions, HAS and combined late puzzles. All levels are selectable; retain completed-page marks while navigating the running game. Connect title, world/level selection, play, completion and next-page/final-campaign navigation.
- **R6 — Presentation.** Use coherent whimsical illustrated art: each noun has a distinct recognizable sprite, word tiles have styled textured backgrounds and legible typography, and the board has textured terrain. Distinguish nouns, verbs and properties visually; show active words, controlled objects, goals and hazards. Animate tile movement, rule activation, victory and screen transitions. At 1280 by 720, the board, active-rule list, movement controls and outcomes must be readable; at 390 by 844 a scrollable large board is allowed. Pure default widgets, solid rectangles or color swaps do not satisfy full presentation credit.
- **R7 — Testable engine.** Export `Rewrite`, `NOUNS`, `PROPERTIES`, `LEVELS` from `game.js`. `new Rewrite(level)` accepts a campaign ID or an independent level record for acceptance fixtures. A record has optional `width`, `height`, and `id`, `name`, `world`, `hint`, `sentences`, `words`, `objects`, `walls`. A sentence is `[text,x,y,vertical]`; each token becomes a movable word block, with horizontal orientation by default. Loose words are `[text,x,y]`, objects are `[noun,x,y]`, terrain walls are `[x,y]`. Expose `level`, `words`, `objects`, `rules`, `activeWords`, `history`, `moves`, `state`, `completed`, and `load(level)`, `move(dx,dy)`, `undo()`, `reset()`, `properties(object)`. Words expose `id`, `text`, `x`, `y`; objects expose `id`, `noun`, `x`, `y`; rules expose `subject`, `condition`, `verb`, `target`, `words`. Properties and active-word IDs are sets; state is `playing`, `won` or `stranded`. The UI must use the same engine. Keep the supplied layouts, but do not hard-code their solutions or predicates; private fixtures use other arrangements.
- **R8 — UI and demos.** Use visible `#start`, `#levels`, `#level-N`, `#level-name`, `#board`, `#rules`, `#controlled`, `#moves`, `#status`, `#undo`, `#reset`, `#up`, `#down`, `#left`, `#right`, `#completion`, `#next`. Word elements expose `data-word` and boolean `data-active`; object elements expose `data-noun` and space-separated `data-properties`. Z undoes and R resets. Support `?level=` and deterministic scenarios `?scenario=handoff` (level 6), `?scenario=inheritance` (level 17), `?scenario=near-victory` (level 1 after Up,Right,Right). Include 1–10 real input traces in `demo_outputs/`, indexed by `demos.json`, demonstrating normal play and a later rule interaction. Each has optional `scenario`, `duration_frames` up to 600 and ordered `events` with `frame`, `type`, pointer `x`, `y`, `button` or keyboard `keycode`. Playback is deterministic at 30 frames per second and 1280 by 720.
- **R9 — Full assessment.** Retain the launch gate and all 14 criteria in `rubric.md`: mechanics 15%, content depth 35%, functional visuals 15%, presentation/art 35%. Automated checks cover all campaign solutions and independent cases for parsing, pushing, multiple control, conditions, transformations, inheritance, outcome priority and long undo history, plus real keyboard/UI and demo replay. Visual quality, content distinction and animation polish require recorded review; unreviewed scores remain unset.
