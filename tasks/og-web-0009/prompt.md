# Potion Shop

Build a complete cozy browser apothecary with recipe-based brewing,automatic customers,pricing,freshness,reputation,restocking,upgrades and a seven-day business cycle. Deliver a self-contained local `index.html`;the supplied fantasy and apothecary atlases are optional artwork.

- **R1 — Ingredients and recipes.** Start with 45 gold,60 reputation,day 1,empty potion shelves and one freshness-3 batch of each ingredient at the quantities below. Initially know only heal and cure. Select exactly 2 ingredient units,heat gentle/steady/hot,and an integer stir count from 0 through 5. Repeated ingredients are allowed experiments. Each recipe uses one unit of each listed ingredient,independent of selection order. Show known recipes and required procedure;unknown recipes remain unrevealed in the journal until discovered by successfully making them or buying their book. Reject unavailable ingredients,invalid selections,wrong phase,an occupied cauldron or full shelves without partial consumption. Consume oldest/least-fresh batches first,breaking equal freshness by batch creation order. Starting a brew consumes ingredients immediately. A normal brew lasts 2.5 seconds and creates its bottle only on completion. An unmatched ingredient pair consumes the ingredients and becomes waste. A matching pair makes its potion even with a poor procedure and discovers that recipe once.

- **R2 — Quality,space and aging.** Quality equals minimum consumed freshness plus 2,minus 2 for wrong heat,minus the smaller of 2 and the absolute stir-count error;clamp to 1–5. Show bottle quality and ingredient batch freshness. Shelves initially hold 6 bottles. Give every batch and bottle a unique ID. On each next morning,reduce existing ingredient freshness by 1 to a minimum of 0;increment bottle age and reduce quality by 1 to a minimum of 1. Bottles reaching age 3 are removed and count as waste. During morning/evening the player may discard oldest ingredient units or individual bottles to manage space/freshness;discarded bottles count as waste,ingredient disposal gives no refund. Reject impossible disposal without changes. Do not bypass inventory with infinite ingredients or free bottles.
- **R3 — Day phases and automatic customers.** Morning allows brewing;opening sales requires no active brew. Afternoon customers arrive every 4 seconds,starting immediately,and browse for 2 seconds before automatically buying a matching bottle. Use the customer order knight,gardener,apprentice,merchant,rotated by day minus 1 and repeated as needed. Every customer has independent patience and status. The oldest matching bottle is considered first. A sale requires price no greater than budget and no greater than floor of base price multiplied by quality divided by 5 multiplied by 1.4. Otherwise the customer waits until patience expires;stock still missing means out-of-stock rejection,and matching stock without an affordable offer means too-expensive rejection. A manual recommendation may resolve a waiting customer sooner;wrong-type recommendations cause rejection without consuming the bottle or paying gold. Missing stock recommendations are rejected without changing that customer. Each sale removes exactly one bottle,credits its current price and resolves the customer once. Sales close automatically after all scheduled visitors resolve,or at 60 seconds;remaining visitors are rejected at closing. Evening allows supply purchases,books and upgrades. Close the ledger to see the day report,then advance to the next morning;after day 7,advance to weekly results. Preserve state across days.

- **R4 — Prices,reputation and demand.** Potion prices default to base prices and accept integers 1–60;changes affect waiting customers and subsequent demand. Reputation stays within 0–100. A sale changes reputation by quality minus 3,plus 2 if price is at most 0.8 times base,minus 2 if above base,plus the decor bonus;clamp that change to −3 through 4. Wrong recommendations lose 4 reputation;too-expensive rejections lose 2;out-of-stock rejections lose 3. At opening,visitor count is 6 plus floor of (reputation minus 50) divided by 20,plus 2 if the mean of the four price/base ratios is at most 0.8,or minus 2 if it exceeds 1.2;clamp count to 4–12. Freeze that day's count after opening. Lower prices must therefore change actual future foot traffic,not just labels. Show needs,budgets,patience,purchase/disappointment feedback and reputation consequences.
- **R5 — Supplies,books and improvements.** In evening buy 1–20 units of a selected ingredient at its listed unit cost,creating a new freshness-3 batch. Buy an unknown recipe book for 15 gold;already-known books cannot be bought again. Purchases require enough gold and must not partially apply. Include all upgrades below,each purchasable once. New garden ingredients arrive after existing stock ages on each subsequent morning. Track all supplier/book/upgrade spending separately from revenue. The player can use proceeds to improve future capacity and efficiency.

- **R6 — Complete presentation and records.** Connect an illustrated title,morning brewing,afternoon sales,evening supplier,daily reports and weekly results without restarting. Daily records show brewed/sold/unhappy counts,revenue,spending,waste,new recipes,opening/closing gold and reputation. Weekly results aggregate those counts and show days operated,remaining gold,reputation,known recipes,upgrades and the seven-day ledger. A fresh shop resets all progress. Use a consistent warm magical interior with real illustrated shelves,wooden surfaces,herbs,lighting,cauldron,recognizable ingredient/potion sprites and distinct customer portraits/ailment icons. Provide bubbling brewing,bottle creation/glow,customer arrival and purchase/disappointment,ingredient sparkle,visible restocking and updated price tags. Recipe cards,menus and HUD must be themed. At 1280 by 720 the main shop areas and current controls must be clear and non-overlapping;long recipe journals,queues and suppliers may scroll. At 390 by 844 scrolling is allowed.
