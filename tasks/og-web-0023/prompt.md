# Connect the level camera, player and menus

Complete `component.js` in the supplied local browser scene in `game/`; retain `index.html`. The package supplies SceneObject, Camera, the player factory, complete menu factory/handler, transition material and perspective projection. This task assembles the level and binds the provided instances.

- **R1 — Assembly.** Export `assembleLevel(root, {Camera, createPlayer, createMenus})`. Call each supplied factory once. Add the returned player, a new supplied Camera instance named LevelCamera, and the returned menu instance as direct siblings under root. Return `{player, camera, menus}` with those exact objects. Preserve the player's PlayerCamera child and the complete menu component; do not reconstruct either factory result.
- **R2 — Static camera transform.** Set camera position to [-12.2164, 15.8305, 12.4355], within a Euclidean tolerance of 0.05. Set its three basis columns to [0.794097, 0, 0.607792], [0.37447, 0.787656, -0.489255] and [-0.478731, 0.616115, 0.625475], each within 0.01. Store columns in that order in `basis`. These are camera-local axes in world space, not Euler angles. Leave `current = false` when assembly returns; the supplied handler activates it on start.
- **R3 — Bindings.** Assign the player instance itself to `menus.playerNode` and the camera itself to `menus.levelCamera`. Preserve the supplied menu's `transitionMaterial` object identity and all existing methods, panel references and buttons. The player and menus must share the level root.
- **R4 — Visible integration.** Preserve the supplied start behavior: the menu handler hides the player, activates LevelCamera, pauses gameplay and runs the 2-second overlay fade while paused. Render the scene through the active camera, using both its position and basis. Play performs its 2-second transition, shows the player, switches to PlayerCamera and resumes time/motion. Pause/Resume remains usable; Show death switches back to the level camera and hides the player. Keep the supplied material-driven transition and controls.
- **R5 — Lifecycle and browser behavior.** Restart and Reset reconstruct a clean level with one player, one static camera and one menu instance, maintaining the factory links and fresh references. Preserve browser fullscreen toggle and the local Quit transition to Session ended. Do not replace camera rendering with a label or a fixed screenshot.

Acceptance injects alternate factories to verify exact instance/parent/material identity, checks the position and basis separately, compares projected landmark coordinates against independent camera math, exercises player/menu camera switching and paused operation, and verifies clean reconstruction. The public example checks the starting menu camera. Do not modify checks or fabricate results.
