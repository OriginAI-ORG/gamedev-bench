# Border Check

Build a complete browser document-inspection simulation with ten timed workdays,escalating rules,a household budget and consequential moral choices. Deliver a self-contained local `index.html`. Preserve the supplied `campaign.js`:it contains the authoritative people,rules,eight-person daily rosters and story choices. It is content data,not an inspection/finance implementation. The supplied illustrated border atlas is optional.

- **R1 — Inspectable workspace.** Show the current traveler,portrait,nationality,purpose,story,baggage weight,queue,desk,rulebook and APPROVE/DENY stamps. Documents can be dragged by their headers and layered within the desk; fields can be manually highlighted for comparison without revealing the correct answer. Match the passport photograph to the real traveler portrait. Include distinct passport,entry/work permit,vaccination card,entry ticket and customs declaration designs with their different fields. Show all currently required documents and a scrollable cumulative rulebook. Search baggage from day 9 to reveal its contents; before searching show sealed baggage. Stamping shows an ink effect,traveler reaction and any citation; prevent double stamping,then allow the next traveler. Decisions must be computed from actual document fields and current rules,not a roster's defect label.
- **R2 — Ten cumulative rule sets.** Day dates are 2030-05-01 through 2030-05-10. Apply all earlier rules as well as the new rule below. Equal-to-today expiry is valid. The supplied campaign's default passport/permit/vaccine expiry is 2030-05-30; expired cases use the previous day. A clean traveler has a passport ID formed from the person's first 2 ID letters uppercased,hyphen,and 100 plus day; permits and vaccines cross-reference that ID. Normal permits match the stated purpose; medical travelers have a physician seal. Default baggage is 10 kg declared and measured,with books. Weight-boundary cases measure 12 kg; mismatches measure 14 kg. Present passports from day 1,permits from day 4,vaccine cards from day 6,tickets from day 7,and customs declarations from day 8,except explicit missing-document cases. Preserve the provided rosters,characters and story sequence. Build fresh document objects; changing one traveler must not alter another.

|Day|Additional rule|
|---|---|
|1|Passport required. Passport name equals the traveler name; names on all presented documents agree with that name.|
|2|Passport photo matches the traveler.|
|3|Passport has not expired.|
|4|Permit required and not expired.|
|5|Permit number is nonempty; its passport ID and purpose match the passport and traveler.|
|6|Vaccination card required with matching passport ID,C17 vaccine and unexpired validity.|
|7|Entry ticket required,dated today,with a nonempty serial.|
|8|Customs declaration required; absolute declared/measured weight difference is at most 2 kg.|
|9|Weapons prohibited; books,food and medicine allowed. Searching exposes the baggage contents.|
|10|Rusk nationals denied unless purpose is medical and their permit has a physician seal.|

- **R3 — Timed wages and citations.** Normal startup opens a title,then day 1 briefing. Each shift starts at 90 seconds with 8 queued travelers. Countdown continues in inspection and stamped-decision states; it pauses elsewhere. An approval is correct only if there are no violations; denying any traveler with a violation is correct. A wrong approval or wrong denial adds 1 citation. Each decided traveler adds 1 processed. After the last decision's Next action or timer expiry,close the shift. Unprocessed travelers earn nothing. Daily earnings are maximum of 0 and processed multiplied by 10 minus citations multiplied by 8; credit them once. Timer expiry cannot stamp another traveler. State transitions must not duplicate wages,citations or decisions.
- **R4 — Family survival.** Start with 25 savings,mother and child each at 5/5 health,no illness and no rent arrears. At the start of day 4 the child becomes ill; at day 8 the mother becomes ill. The report lets the player choose rent 15,heat 5,food 10 and medicine 8 when anyone is ill. Medicine is unnecessary and free when nobody is ill. Reject unaffordable allocations without applying any payment or health change. Missing food costs each member 1 health; missing heat also costs 1. Each untreated ill member loses 2 health; paid medicine cures all currently ill members. If food and heat are paid and a member is healthy after treatment,restore 1 health up to 5. Clamp health to 0. Paying rent resets consecutive arrears; missing it twice consecutively causes eviction. Any member reaching 0 or eviction immediately produces the family-loss ending before the story event. Deduct the selected costs once and preserve family state across days.
- **R5 — Recurring stories and endings.** Use all 10 events and both choices from the supplied data. Marek,Irena and Sera recur as travelers and after-hours characters. Apply the specified costs,gold,resistance,loyalty,corruption and escape-paper effects; corruption cannot become negative. Refuse unaffordable choices without progress or partial changes. Display the choice's consequence,updated money/story totals and recent history. Day 8 escape papers cost 15; day 10 escape additionally requires those papers and 120 savings,and deducts the fare. Escaping produces the escape ending. Staying after day 10 produces resistance if resistance is at least 5,otherwise compliance. Family loss has priority before these events. Bribes,refugee assistance,corruption reporting and resistance choices must affect resources or accumulated state,not just text. Each event can resolve once. Show distinct illustrated ending scenes,household fate,processed/citation totals,savings and a review of daily reports and choices. Restart clears all progress.
- **R6 — Reports,flow and presentation.** Connect title,briefing,inspection,report,story,next day and endings without a reload. Every report records processed travelers,citations,net earnings,selected/paid expenses,family health/illness and narrative consequences. The original bleak bureaucratic mood must carry through desk,documents,portraits,handbook,stamps,home,story and reports using authored illustration or pixel art. Animate traveler approach/response,document arrival,stamping,citation emphasis and handbook/page transitions. Use readable themed widgets and period-style typography. At 1280 by 720,documents,traveler,stamps,queue,timer and rulebook occupy readable distinct areas; overlapping documents remain movable. The handbook may scroll. At 390 by 844 scrolling is allowed and actions remain usable.
- **R7 — Testable model.** Export `BorderRun`, `traveler`, `violations`, `dateFor`, `PEOPLE`, `RULES`, `ROSTERS`, `EVENTS`, `EXPENSES` from `game.js`. `traveler(person,fault,day)` builds fresh inspectable records from the supplied roster; `violations(record,day)` returns violation descriptions. A record includes `person`, `name`, `nation`, `face`, `purpose`, `story`, `docs`, `measured`, `cargo`, `searched`; document keys are `passport`, `permit`, `vaccine`, `ticket`, `customs` with the fields shown in the rules. `new BorderRun(scenario)` exposes `screen`, `day`, `money`, `family`, `rentMisses`, `resistance`, `loyalty`, `corruption`, `escapePass`, `queue`, `index`, `current`, `rules`, `time`, `processed`, `citations`, `report`, `reports`, `history`, `lastDecision`, `result`, `message`. Family fields are `mother`, `child`, `motherIll`, `childIll`. Methods are `start()`, `begin()`, `search()`, `decide(approve)`, `next()`, `step(seconds)`, `finishDay()`, `cost(choice)`, `settle(choice)`, `choice(option)`, `summary()`. Expense choices contain booleans `rent`, `heat`, `food`, `medicine`; event options are `a`, `b`. Screens are `title`, `briefing`, `inspection`, `decision`, `report`, `event`, `ending`. The UI must use this model. Acceptance varies independent fields,day boundaries,decision order and elapsed time; complete-campaign tests use actual rosters and legal budget/story choices.
- **R8 — Scenarios and controls.** Support `?scenario=day-6`, `?scenario=day-9`, `?scenario=day-10` at the corresponding first traveler,normal savings/family and full shift; `?scenario=near-timeout` at day 1 with 0.5 seconds remaining; `?scenario=report` after day 1 with 4 processed and 1 citation; `?scenario=family-loss` at the day 1 report with 0 money and both members at 1 health; `?scenario=escape` at the final event with 150 savings and papers; `?scenario=resistance` at the final event with resistance 5. Preserve visible selectors `#start`, `#begin`, `#day`, `#money`, `#timer`, `#processed`, `#citations`, `#traveler-name`, `#queue`, `#weight`, `#cargo`, `#search`, `#desk`, `#doc-TYPE`, `#field-TYPE-FIELD`, `#rules`, `#approve`, `#deny`, `#next`, `#stamp`, `#decision`, `#report-processed`, `#report-citations`, `#earnings`, `#expense-KEY`, `#expenses`, `#remaining`, `#settle`, `#family`, `#choice-a`, `#choice-b`, `#story-state`, `#paid-report`, `#status`, `#outcome`, `#ending-kind`, `#total-processed`, `#total-citations`, `#restart`. Include 1–10 real input traces under `demo_outputs/`,indexed by `demos.json`,covering normal inspection,an advanced rule,budget/story progression and an ending. Traces use optional `scenario`, `duration_frames` at most 600,and ordered `events` with `frame`, `type`,pointer `x`, `y`, `button` or keyboard `keycode`. Replay deterministically at 30 frames per second,1280 by 720.
- **R9 — Full assessment.** Preserve the launch gate and all 18 criteria in `rubric.md`,weighted mechanics 15%,depth 35%,functional visuals 15%,presentation/art 35%. Automatic checks cover rule boundaries,actual document fields,drag/marks,citations/timing,wages,budgets,illness,story effects,four endings,full campaigns and real input demos. Art quality,animation and moral/strategic balance require evidence-based manual review; functional passing does not assign unreviewed scores. The fixed supplied campaign removes open-ended content authoring and the exact formulas/interfaces constrain design choices; these additions support reproducibility and do not establish identical creative difficulty.
