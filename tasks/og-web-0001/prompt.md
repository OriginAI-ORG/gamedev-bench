# Idle Factory Planet

Build a complete browser factory game: freely place machines, connect real resource flows, research technology, exhaust a planet and prestige to a larger one. Provide a themed title and an illustrated industrial game world. The supplied art atlas is optional. Deliver a local, self-contained `index.html` package with no external services.

- **R1 — Spatial construction.** Use a 10 by 6 grid. Players can drag machines from a dock or select a tool and click an empty cell. Support conveyors and independently placed miners, smelters, fabricators and sellers. Each cell has one machine, a directional output and visible production state. Output directions rotate right, down, left, up; support rotating existing cells, salvaging and upgrading machines. Placement must reject occupied/out-of-bounds cells, locked technology and unaffordable purchases.
- **R2 — Connected production.** Run production continuously during play. Miners create ore; smelters consume 2 ore for 1 metal; fabricators consume 1 metal for 1 part; sellers consume 1 part for 12 credits times the permanent multiplier. Base processing times are respectively 1, 1.5, 1.8 and 0.5 seconds. Items move through directed adjacent conveyors, taking at least 0.35 seconds per cell. A disconnected output blocks further production. Standard machine input capacity is 4 items. Show moving resources, machine activity and measured ore/metal/parts/credit throughput so bottlenecks can be identified.
- **R3 — Economy and upgrades.** Begin with 1000 credits. Base prices are miner 30, smelter 50, fabricator 90, seller 70, conveyor 2, storage 60 and advanced fabricator 150. A type's next placement price is its base price times 1.22 raised to the number of existing machines of that type, rounded up. Salvage returns half the base price, rounded down. Upgrading a machine at level L costs its base price times 1.7 raised to L+1, rounded up; processing speed scales by 1.4 raised to its upgrade level. Use K, M and B for large displayed numbers.
- **R4 — Technology.** Provide at least these 3 distinct research options with costs, dependencies and visible effects: `drive` costs 80 and doubles mining speed; `storage` costs 120, unlocks storage and increases input buffers to 8; `assembly` costs 200, requires both earlier upgrades and unlocks an advanced fabricator consuming 1 metal plus 1 ore to make 2 parts in 1.5 seconds. Storage passes stored resource types onward and can hold 8 items. Research cannot be purchased twice or without its prerequisites/funds.
- **R5 — Depletion and prestige.** The first planet contains 600 ore. Each mined item consumes one unit; output must never duplicate resources or make deposits negative. Below 25% remaining, multiply mining speed by remaining fraction divided by 0.25, with a minimum of 0.2. At depletion, offer prestige with the next multiplier preview. Prestige clears the layout, restores 1000 credits and retains all research; the permanent multiplier doubles and affects production speed and sale value. Planet P has rounded resource capacity 600 times 1.8 raised to P-1. Prevent prestige while resources remain.
- **R6 — Complete presentation.** Connect title,construction,continuous production,research,depletion,prestige and the next planet without restarting. Use distinct illustrated machines, textured planet terrain, visible conveyors/resources, themed research panels, resource icons and readable counters. Animate machine operation, item movement, research and planet transitions. Show current planet, credits, remaining resources, permanent multiplier and per-stage rates. At 1280 by 720 all essential regions must be readable; at 390 by 844 scrolling is allowed. Retain pause, a 1 second inspection step and title navigation without making manual stepping the normal production loop.
- **R7 — Testable simulation.** Export `Factory`, `TYPES`, `RESEARCH` and `formatNumber` from `game.js`. A factory exposes `cells`, `credits`, `ore`, `oreTotal`, `planet`, `multiplier`, `research`, `totalSales`, `time`, `paused`, plus `place(x,y,type,dir)`, `remove(x,y)`, `rotate(x,y)`, `upgrade(x,y)`, `buyResearch(id)`, `step(dt)`, `advance(seconds)`, `rates()` and `prestige()`. The UI must use this same simulation. Cell records expose type, coordinates, direction, level, input buffer, output item and production count. Provide deterministic scenarios `empty`, `chain-ready`, `near-depletion`, `researched` through `?scenario=`. The chain scenario places miner,conveyor,smelter,conveyor,fabricator,conveyor,seller across columns 0–6 of row 2, all facing right; near-depletion starts that chain with 1 ore. The researched scenario also unlocks all technology and sets credits to 1000.
- **R8 — Inputs and demos.** Use visible controls `#start`, `#tool-miner`, `#tool-smelter`, `#tool-fabricator`, `#tool-seller`, `#tool-belt`, `#tool-storage`, `#tool-advanced`, `#cell-x-y`, `#direction`, `#rotate`, `#upgrade`, `#remove`, `#research-drive`, `#research-storage`, `#research-assembly`, `#prestige`, `#pause`, `#step`, `#title`. Show `#planet`, `#credits`, `#ore`, `#multiplier`, `#rate-ore`, `#rate-metal`, `#rate-parts`, `#rate-credits` and `#status`. Include 1–10 input traces in `demo_outputs/` and list them in `demos.json`, demonstrating production and prestige. Each uses optional `scenario`, `duration_frames` up to 600 and ordered `events` with `frame`, `type`, pointer `x`, `y`, `button` or keyboard `keycode`. Replay is deterministic at 30 frames per second and 1280 by 720, using real mouse/keyboard input.
- **R9 — Full assessment.** Keep the complete-game scoring scope: mechanics 15%, content depth 35%, functional visuals 15%, presentation/art 35%, gated by successful browser launch. Individual criteria are in `rubric.md`. Automated checks verify routing,consumption,economy,technology,depletion,prestige,continuous UI production and demos; aesthetic quality and the clarity of the progression remain reviewed criteria, not automatic points.
