# Pipe Crisis

Build a complete browser pipe-routing game with time pressure, multiple incompatible fluids, special pipes and a 15-level facility campaign. Deliver a local self-contained `index.html` package, without external services. The supplied industrial atlas is optional.

- **R1 — Construction.** Use a 7 by 5 playable grid, coordinates x=0–6 and y=0–4, surrounded by source/drain terminals. Select pipe pieces from a finite visible inventory and place them in empty cells. Rotate before placement or after selecting a dry placed pipe; return dry pipes to inventory. Reject occupied, blocked, out-of-bounds and out-of-stock placement. Fixed or already wet pipes cannot rotate or be removed. A retry restores the complete initial board, inventory, countdown and fluid state.
- **R2 — Connections.** Directions 0,1,2,3 mean right,down,left,up. At rotation 0, `straight` and `reservoir` open right/left; `corner` opens right/down; `tee` opens right/down/left; `cross` opens all directions. Rotation advances all openings clockwise. Fluid can enter only an open port and exits every other connected opening. A T branches into both exits. Every branch must remain sealed: disconnected ends, blocked entry and out-of-board leaks fail. Crosses separate horizontal and vertical channels and preserve direction without mixing. Ordinary pipes mix all their openings; different fluids sharing one channel cause contamination. Fluids must reach drains of exactly the matching fluid identity.
- **R3 — Timing and distinct fluids.** The displayed planning countdown decreases in real time and automatically starts flow at 0; a start-flow button begins early. Standard red chemicals and blue coolant take 0.5 seconds per traversed cell. Green acid also takes 0.5 seconds, but destroys an unlined pipe 0.3 seconds after entering, causing corrosion failure even if it has already left. Steam takes 0.2 seconds per cell. A reservoir adds 1.5 seconds to outgoing travel, buying construction time. Provide `acid-straight`, `acid-corner` and `acid-cross`, with the corresponding openings and corrosion-resistant lining. Acid crosses retain independent channels. Show fluid advancing, remaining pressure time, corrosion warnings and the failure location.
- **R4 — Complete campaign.** Include the following 15 selectable levels with escalating facilities. Each source is written as fluid(x,y,direction), with R=red, B=blue, A=acid, S=steam. Drain notation omits direction. Inventory abbreviations are s=straight,c=corner,t=tee,x=cross,v=reservoir,a=acid-straight,k=acid-corner,z=acid-cross. Fixed pipes do not consume inventory. Unlisted cells are empty. Levels 11 and 15 additionally require fluid to traverse a reservoir; merely placing an unused reservoir does not satisfy the objective.

- **R5 — Outcomes and progression.** Win only after every matching drain fills, all active branches finish without failure and required special components have actually carried fluid. A partially filled system must not win. Show completion time and routing efficiency: distinct wet cells divided by placed pipe cells, as a rounded percentage. Failure identifies overflow, bad connection, contamination, corrosion, wrong drain or trapped pressure and offers retry. Support title, facility/level select, planning, flow, success, failure and next-level navigation. Preserve completion marks while navigating the running game. The final success returns to level selection.
- **R6 — Presentation.** Use illustrated metallic pipes with recognizable openings, joints and linings; mechanical sources/drains; industrial terrain, gauges and themed UI. Animate flowing liquid with directional motion/bubbles, placement/rotation feedback, overflow and transitions. Display current level, inventory, countdown, filled drains and a legend explaining all fluids. At 1280 by 720 the main board and essential controls/results must remain readable; at 390 by 844 scrolling is allowed. A color swap alone is insufficient to show distinct component/fluid content.
- **R7 — Testable simulation.** Export `Plumbing`, `LEVELS`, `PIPES`, `FLUIDS` and `openings` from `game.js`. `new Plumbing(levelId,scenario)` exposes `level`, `cells`, `stock`, `countdown`, `state`, `elapsed`, `flowTime`, `fronts`, `wet`, `filled`, `failure`, `completed`, and methods `place(x,y,type,rot)`, `rotate(x,y)`, `remove(x,y)`, `start()`, `step(dt)`, `advance(seconds)`, `efficiency()` and `load(id)`. Cells contain `x`, `y`, `type`, `rot`, `locked`. States are `planning`, `flow`, `won`, `failed`; failed input returns false. The interface must use this simulation. Level data exposes `sources`, `drains`, `stock`, `blocks`, `fixed` and optional `required`; acceptance may substitute fluid identities or stock values to verify general routing. Support `?level=` plus deterministic `?scenario=near-victory` (level 5 with the straight horizontal/vertical paths and separated center cross ready) and `?scenario=overflow` (empty level 1 with 0.1 seconds before flow).
- **R8 — Controls and input demos.** Use visible `#start`, `#levels`, `#level-N`, `#tool-TYPE`, `#stock-TYPE`, `#cell-x-y`, `#rotate-preview`, `#rotate`, `#remove`, `#flow`, `#retry`, `#next`, `#board`, `#countdown`, `#drains`, `#status`, `#result`, `#reason`, `#efficiency`, `#elapsed`. R rotates the selected pipe or pending piece. Include 1–10 real input traces in `demo_outputs/`, indexed by `demos.json`, covering routing success and failure/retry. Each has optional `scenario`, `duration_frames` up to 600 and ordered `events` with `frame`, `type`, pointer `x`, `y`, `button` or keyboard `keycode`. Replay is deterministic at 30 frames per second and 1280 by 720.
- **R9 — Full assessment.** Keep the launch gate and all 14 criteria in `rubric.md`: mechanics 15%, content depth 35%, functional visuals 15%, presentation/art 35%. Automatic checks verify all levels, routing failures, isolation, corrosion, timing, inventory, live countdown, UI and demos. Art, thematic variety and motion quality require recorded review and remain unscored when unreviewed.
