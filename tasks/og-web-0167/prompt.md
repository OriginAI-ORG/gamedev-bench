# Assemble reusable battle targeting components

Complete the missing detector, target finder, bindings and opposing-group mapping. The provided browser fixture supplies the scene registry, overlap service, unit bodies and visible inspection controls. `config.json` contains this task's group names, base range, attack range, unit identities and positions.

- **R1 — Team mapping.** In `mapping.js`, define `TARGET` so team 0 targets the group at `config.groups[1]` and team 1 targets `config.groups[0]`. The fixture installs it as `UnitStats.TARGET`. Use that mapping when querying the scene registry.
- **R2 — Detection geometry.** Implement `DetectRange` in `component.js` with `colShape`, `baseRangeSize` and a `stats` setter. Every assignment updates collision layer to `4 * (stats.team + 1)` and mask to `2 - stats.team`, and replaces the collider shape with a new circle whose radius is `baseRangeSize * stats.attackRange`. Preserve the supplied unit body's 24 by 24 rectangle and layer `team + 1`; the overlap service uses circle-versus-rectangle contact, not just center distance.
- **R3 — Assembly and updates.** `assemble(unit, scene, config)` attaches `DetectRange` with its `Collider` child and a sibling `TargetFinder` to each unit, exposing them as `detectRange` and `targetFinder`. `bind(unit, config)` in `bindings.js` binds the finder to the actual unit. `applyStats(unit, config)` forwards the unit's current stats to the detector on startup and every stats assignment. The supplied unit setter already clones stats; preserve that isolation.
- **R4 — Range events.** `TargetFinder.ready()` connects to its actor's detector `area-entered` and `area-exited` events. Maintain a separate `targetsInRange` array of actual `BattleUnit` references, ignoring non-unit objects. Add/remove in place and emit `targets-in-range-changed` once for each membership change. Duplicate entry or absent exit must not duplicate membership or events. `hasTargetInRange()` reports whether the array is nonempty.
- **R5 — Closest opposing target.** `findTarget()` queries `scene.getNodesInGroup()` with the mapped opposing group and stores the closest live `BattleUnit` in `target`, using squared global-position distance. Search the entire opposing group, including units outside the detection circle. Ignore non-unit objects and the actor itself. Keep registry order on ties and clear the target when no candidate exists.
- **R6 — Rendering and interaction.** Preserve the supplied range ring, target line, selected-unit movement, attack-range selector and closer/outside/remove/reset controls. The visible ring, target and count must reflect actual components. Moving a unit or changing stats must cause real overlap membership updates; the target can remain selected when every opponent is outside range.
- **R7 — Lifecycle.** Each unit has independent detector state, shape and target array. `dispose()` removes component listeners and clears the finder's target/list without affecting other units. Repeated cleanup is safe.

Edit `component.js`, `bindings.js` and `mapping.js`; keep the other supplied files unchanged. Submit the complete package with `index.html` as entry. Acceptance checks both teams, exact per-task parameters, scene structure, typed events, shape replacement, inherited coordinates, out-of-range nearest targets, live overlap boundaries, rendering and cleanup.
