It replaces the original runtime's scene assembly while preserving the scene data, attachment and discovery obligations. The pickup renderer is supplied WebGL code; shader/renderer construction is not an added solver task. The end-to-end fixture probes are additions for observing the restored component contracts, not replacement core requirements.
